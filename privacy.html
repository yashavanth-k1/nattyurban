Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

NATTYURBAN is an online clothing brand based in India. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website or contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using this website, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

Right now this website is a launch page. It does not ask you to create an account, place an order or fill in any form. The information we may receive depends on how you interact with us:

  • Messages you send us. If you email or WhatsApp us, we receive your name, email address or phone number, and whatever you choose to share in your message.
  • Basic visit data. Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log technical details such as your IP address, browser type, device type and the pages you open.
  • Analytics and advertising tools. If we add tools such as Google Analytics or Meta Pixel, they may use cookies or similar technologies to count visits and measure clicks, for example on our Instagram button.
  • Orders (once our online store is live). When you place an order we will collect the details needed to deliver it: your name, delivery address, phone number, email address and order details.

2. How we use your information

  • To reply to your questions and messages.
  • To process, ship and support your orders once the online store is live.
  • To send launch news and offers, only where you have asked for them or agreed to receive them.
  • To understand how people use our website and improve it.
  • To keep our website secure and to meet legal requirements.

3. Payments

When online ordering opens, payments will be handled by third-party payment providers. We do not see or store your full card, UPI PIN or net banking details on our own systems. Those providers have their own privacy policies.

4. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the business, such as website hosting, payment providers, courier and delivery partners, and analytics tools. We share only what they need to do their job. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

5. Cookies and third-party links

Cookies are small files stored on your device. You can block or delete them in your browser settings, though parts of a website may then work differently. Our website links to Instagram (@nattyurban.co). When you follow that link you leave our website, and Instagram's own privacy policy applies to what happens there.

6. How long we keep your information

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as long as the law requires, for example for tax and accounting records on orders. After that we delete it or make it anonymous.

7. How we protect your information

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold, including using a secure (HTTPS) website and limiting who can access it. No method of sending or storing data online is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights

Under Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may have the right to:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct or update it;
  • ask us to delete it;
  • withdraw your consent to messages and offers at any time.

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Children

Our website is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when our online store opens. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed. Please check this page occasionally.
